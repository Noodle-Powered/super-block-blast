Privacy Policy for Super Block Blast

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Super Block Blast ("the game", "we", "us") is published by Noodle Powered. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game handles and how.

Information we handle

We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal details to play.

  • Game data stored on your device. Your progress, settings, scores and purchased in-game items are saved locally on your device so the game works between sessions. We do not upload this data to our own servers.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase succeeded so we can grant the corresponding in-game items.
  • Google Play Games Services (if you use it). If the game offers leaderboards or achievements, Google Play Games Services may use your Play Games profile and a player identifier to record and display your scores. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising (if shown). The game may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. See Google's policies below for details and opt-out options.

How information is used

We use the information above only to run the game: save your progress, deliver items you purchase, show leaderboards/achievements where available, and (where applicable) display ads.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is handled by the Google services described above (Google Play, Play Games Services, AdMob) under Google's own terms.

Children

This game is not directed at children under the age required by law in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can remove locally stored game data by uninstalling the game.
  • You can reset or limit ad personalisation in your device's settings under "Ads" / advertising identifier.

Third-party services

This game relies on services provided by Google, including Google Play, Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob. Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
