1. Who we are

The Game is published by Noodle Powered, the data controller responsible for your information under this policy. For any privacy question or request, contact us at noodlepoweredgames@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account or give us your name to play. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on how you use the Game and the features you enable, the following information may be collected:

(a) Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, settings, preferences and purchased in-game items are saved locally on your device so the Game works between sessions. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our own servers.

(b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information such as device model, operating system and version, language, region, and general performance/crash information used to keep the Game running properly.
  • Identifiers such as a mobile advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA on iOS) and similar device identifiers, where applicable to advertising or analytics.
  • Usage information such as levels reached, sessions, features used and in-app events, used in aggregate to understand and improve gameplay.

(c) Information you choose to provide

  • Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message so we can respond.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional). If the Game offers leaderboards or achievements and you sign in, Google Play Games Services provides a player identifier and your Play Games profile to record and display scores.

(d) Purchase information

When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive confirmation of the purchase and the product purchased, but we do not receive or store your full payment card number or billing details.
