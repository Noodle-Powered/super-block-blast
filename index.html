About this game

Super Block Blast is a relaxing yet addictive block puzzle. Drag colorful blocks onto the 8×8 grid, complete full rows and columns to clear them, and chain combos for high scores.

Dig out of tight spots with power-ups - Clear Board, Single Block and Reset Pieces - and pick the pace that suits you with Relax, Normal and Hard modes. Beat your best score and keep the board alive!

Features

Classic 8×8 block puzzle Combos & line clears Power-ups 3 difficulty modes Daily rewards Plays offline
